Three Layers (outer → inner):
Photoreceptors (outer ring)
Rods (brown): 800 segments - peripheral regions
Cones (coral): 40 segments - clustered at fovea (top)
Bipolar Cells (middle ring)
Rod Bipolar (brownish-gold): 47 segments - positioned in peripheral regions near rods
Cone Bipolar ON (green): 53 segments - concentrated near foveal cones
Cone Bipolar OFF (red): 67 segments - concentrated near foveal cones
RGCs (inner ring)
Gold: 15 segments
Connections (based on anatomical convergence):
Connection	Ratio	Visualization
Rods → Rod Bipolar	~20-40:1	Brown lines, 8 nearest rods per bipolar
Cones → Cone Bipolar ON	~1-15:1	Green lines, 6 nearest cones
Cones → Cone Bipolar OFF	~1-15:1	Red lines, 6 nearest cones
Bipolars → RGCs	~15-25:1	Gold lines, 8 nearest bipolars per RGC
Key anatomical features:
Cone bipolars clustered near fovea: ~70% of cone bipolars are positioned in the angular region corresponding to the cones
Rod bipolars in periphery: Distributed where rods dominate
Interleaved ON/OFF: Cone bipolar ON and OFF are interleaved in the foveal region, representing parallel pathways
The connection lines show the convergence pattern - many photoreceptors feeding into fewer bipolars, which then converge onto even fewer RGCs.
